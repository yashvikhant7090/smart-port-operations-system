## 🎯 Problem Statement

The 2021 LA/Long Beach port backlog had 100+ ships waiting offshore for weeks, costing global supply chains more than $10B.

Port operators currently allocate berths, cranes, and yard space across hundreds of vessels manually using spreadsheets. Congestion hotspots are often identified reactively, after vessels are already queuing, and alternate routing decisions come too late to help.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**PortOptima** is an AI-powered port operations optimisation system designed to predict container congestion before it becomes critical and help port operators make faster operational decisions.

The platform analyses vessel, berth, crane, yard, and congestion data to provide actionable recommendations.

### Key Capabilities

- 🚢 **Vessel Monitoring**
- 📊 **Container Congestion Prediction**
- 🅿️ **Berth Allocation Optimisation**
- 🏗️ **Crane Resource Optimisation**
- 📦 **Yard Space Management**
- ⚠️ **Congestion Hotspot Detection**
- 🔄 **Alternate Routing Recommendations**
- 🤖 **AI-powered Operational Recommendations**
- 📈 **Real-time Analytics Dashboard**

📊 Core Modules
1. Vessel Management

Monitor vessel arrivals, departures, waiting vessels, estimated arrival times, and vessel status.

2. Congestion Prediction

Predict potential congestion using vessel flow, port capacity, berth availability, yard utilisation, and operational data.

3. Berth Optimisation

Recommend suitable berths based on vessel requirements, availability, and expected arrival time.

4. Crane Optimisation

Help allocate cranes efficiently according to vessel workload and operational priority.

5. Yard Management

Monitor container yard capacity and identify areas approaching critical utilisation.

6. Risk Detection

Identify congestion hotspots and classify operational risk levels.

7. Alternate Routing

Provide alternative routing recommendations when congestion or operational constraints are detected.

8. AI Recommendations

Generate practical recommendations that help port operators take faster decisions.
